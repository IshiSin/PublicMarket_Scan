# Alphabet 2026-Q1 — AI Key Takeaways

1. **Alphabet’s 2026 CapEx guidance raised to $180B–$190B, with ~60% of Q1 technical infrastructure spend ($35.7B) allocated to servers powering AI workloads.** The company is prioritizing AI compute and data center capacity to meet unprecedented internal and external demand.

2. **Google Cloud AI solutions revenue surged ~800% YoY in Q1, driving Cloud revenue to $20B (+63%) and backlog to $462B.** Enterprise AI offerings, led by Gemini 3, became the primary growth driver for Cloud for the first time.

3. **Gemini Enterprise paid monthly active users grew 40% QoQ, with 330 Google Cloud customers each processing over 1 trillion tokens in the past 12 months and 35 exceeding 10 trillion tokens.** The platform now powers agentic workflows for global brands including Bosch, Merck, and Mars.

4. **Alphabet is deploying 8th-generation TPUs specialized for training (TPU 8t) and inference (TPU 8i), delivering 3× training performance and 80% better inference performance per dollar versus the prior generation.** TPUs, alongside NVIDIA GPUs (including upcoming Vera Rubin NVL 72), form the core of Alphabet’s AI accelerator portfolio.

5. **TPU hardware sales are being extended to select external customers (capital markets, frontier AI labs, HPC) in their own data centers, with revenue expected to be small in 2026 and the majority recognized in 2027.** The move expands addressable market while maintaining a ROIC-focused allocation strategy under compute constraints.

6. **Gemini models now process over 16 billion tokens per minute via direct API, up from 10 billion last quarter, reflecting accelerating enterprise adoption.** The throughput increase underscores rising demand for first-party model inference at scale.

7. **Generative media models reached major scale: Lyria 3 generated 150M songs since launch, Imagen 2 produced 1B images in nearly half the time of Imagen 1, and Veo 3.1 Lite debuted as the most cost-efficient video model to date.** These models power AI-driven content creation across Google products.

8. **Gemma 4 launched as Alphabet’s most intelligent open model, achieving 50M downloads in weeks and contributing to over 500M total downloads of Alphabet open models.** The release expands access to high-performance, open-weights AI.

9. **Search latency reduced by more than 35% over five years despite adding AI features, and Gemini 3 lowered the cost of core AI responses by over 30%.** Efficiency gains stem from hardware and engineering breakthroughs across the AI stack.

10. **AI Overviews and AI Mode (now Gemini 3-powered) are driving search usage to all-time-high query volumes and expanding monetizable coverage beyond the historical ~20% of queries.** Gemini’s improved intent understanding enables ad relevance gains of nearly 10% in Discover and Maps.

11. **Ads quality improved via Gemini-powered Smart Bidding and AI Max, with advertisers like Hilton EMEA capturing one-third more clicks for one-fifth the spend and increasing average booking value by 55%.** Over 30% of customer Search spend now uses AI-enabled campaigns.

12. **Alphabet is rolling out agentic commerce via the Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP), with Amazon, Meta, Microsoft, Salesforce, and Stripe joining founding members, and Ulta Beauty launching agentic checkout in AI Mode and Gemini.** UCP enables streamlined discovery-to-checkout flows across merchants.

13. **Waymo surpassed 500,000 fully autonomous rides per week in Q1, doubling in less than a year, and expanded to 11 major U.S. cities in 2026.** The fleet growth reflects scaling of physical AI and robotics in transportation.

14. **Compute demand exceeds supply, with Alphabet noting it is “compute-constrained” and that Cloud revenue would have been higher if more capacity were available.** The constraint is expected to persist through most of 2026 until new data center capacity comes online.

15. **2027 CapEx is projected to “significantly increase” versus 2026 to support AI infrastructure, with 2026 already elevated to $180B–$190B (including Intersect acquisition).** The multi-year investment plan targets servers, data centers, and power to meet AI-driven demand.

16. **YouTube Music and Premium non-trial subscribers posted their largest quarterly increase since June 2018, while YouTube Premium Lite launched in 23 countries with plans for 12+ more in Q2.** Subscriptions revenue continues to outpace ads, supported by AI personalization and creator tools.
